It takes about 20–30 minutes on a phone or computer. Have these ready:

- Your plan for the space and how the business will be funded
- For each owner who will personally guarantee the lease: date of birth, Social Security number,

- Optional: business plan, recent tax return or bank statements (photos or PDFs are fine)

At the end, each owner signs with a finger on the screen. The $99 non-refundable application and
screening fee is paid by cash or check payable to "Center Hill Lodge 77" — we can't run the
background and credit check until it's received.

As we discussed: this is a 3-year lease paid in monthly installments, approval is a credit decision
with personal guaranties, and applications are reviewed at the Lodge's meeting on the first Monday
of each month. To move in, you'll need three months' rent at lease signing: the first and last
month's rent plus a security deposit equal to one month's rent.
